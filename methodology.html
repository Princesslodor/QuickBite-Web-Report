Research Methodology

Our evaluation followed the DECIDE Framework, a standard in Human-Computer Interaction, to measure the usability of the QuickBite prototype among 40 PSU students.

The DECIDE Framework Application

  • D: Determine goals — Reach a "Good" SUS score (>68).
  • E: Explore questions — Can Automotive students order in <15 mins?
  • C: Choose methods — SUS Questionnaires and Observation.
  • I: Identify issues — Testing during campus break hours.
  • D: Decide ethics — Ensured 100% voluntary participation.
  • E: Evaluate — Finalizing the mean score of 71.13.
System Usability Scale (SUS)

A 10-item industry-standard tool used to calculate a single usability score from 0-100. It is reliable even with small sample sizes, but our use of 40 respondents ensures high accuracy.

Observation Technique

We recorded "Time-on-Task" to see if users struggled with the navigation flow, followed by qualitative open-ended questions to gather student sentiments.
