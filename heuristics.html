Heuristic Evaluation

Applying Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Principles

Usability Inspection Results

We evaluated the QuickBite prototype against the standard 10 heuristics to identify UI/UX strengths and areas for improvement before conducting the 40-respondent user test.

Heuristic Application in QuickBite Status
1. Visibility of System Status Real-time tracking of "Order Received," "Preparing," and "Ready for Pick-up." Passed
2. Match between System & Real World Uses local PSU canteen menu terms and familiar icons (trash bin for delete). Passed
3. User Control & Freedom "Undo" and "Edit" options in the cart allow students to fix mistakes quickly. Passed
4. Consistency & Standards Follows standard mobile navigation patterns (Bottom Nav Bar). Passed
5. Error Prevention Confirmation pop-ups before placing an order to prevent accidental purchases. Minor Polish
6. Recognition Rather than Recall Visual menu items with images so students don't have to memorize dish names. Passed
7. Flexibility & Efficiency of Use Designated "Quick Order" for top-selling canteen items for busy students. Passed
8. Aesthetic & Minimalist Design Clean white space with "QuickBite Orange" accents to avoid visual clutter. Passed
9. Help Users with Errors Clear error messages if a field is left blank during checkout. Passed
